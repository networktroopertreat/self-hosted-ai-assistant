**🧠 Self Hosted AI Assistant** is the complete English-language guide to running your own AI assistant on your own hardware. Learn how to run local LLMs with Ollama, add a chat UI, give the assistant tools and document memory (RAG), and expose it safely to your phone — with runnable Docker configs and a privacy checklist. Built for developers, privacy-conscious users, and homelab owners who want an assistant that never sends their data to a third party. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
